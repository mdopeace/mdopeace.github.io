# Code of Conduct

This project welcomes respectful discussion and constructive feedback regardless
of background, identity, experience or ability.

## Expected behavior

- Discuss ideas and code respectfully; explain disagreements with evidence.
- Respect privacy, accessibility needs, boundaries and requests to stop contact.
- Give appropriate credit and respect others' intellectual property.
- Accept feedback and help keep discussions focused on the project.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexual harassment, impersonation, deliberate
misrepresentation of authorship, publishing private information, and repeated
disruptive conduct are unacceptable.

## Scope and reporting

These expectations apply to this repository's issues, pull requests and other
project spaces, and to interactions where someone is representing the project.

Report concerns privately to the maintainer, Md Mostafijur Rahman, at
[md.correo.e@gmail.com](mailto:md.correo.e@gmail.com). Include relevant links and
context. Do not publish sensitive allegations or personal information in issues.
Reports involving the maintainer may also be directed through GitHub's platform
reporting mechanisms. Treat reports discreetly; absolute confidentiality cannot
be promised where action or legal obligations require disclosure.

The maintainer may remove content, warn participants, restrict participation or
ban participants according to the circumstances. Retaliation against someone
reporting a concern in good faith is unacceptable. A person affected by a decision
may request reconsideration privately with additional context.

This document governs participation. It does not grant a software license,
transfer copyright, or replace the applicable contribution terms.
